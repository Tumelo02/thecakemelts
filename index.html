I've been Creating Sweet Moments Since 2021

I started The Cake Melts in 2021 as a self-taught baker with a true passion for creativity. What began as a hobby quickly grew into a business built on consistency, detail, and a love for creating special moments. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, anniversary, graduation, baby shower, corporate event, or even a simple craving, each cake I craft is designed to bring joy and melt hearts.

I'm mostly known for my creative variation cakes and cupcakes that are based on customer custom ideas on how they want their cakes/cupcakes to look. Not only are they beautiful, but they are always fresh and delicious.
